Two Lives That Continue to Inspire

A celebration of scholarship, dedication, mentorship and the thousands of engineers whose careers were forged along the way.

Some people teach lessons; others inspire lives. Some impart knowledge; others ignite the passion to discover, innovate, and excel. And a rare few do both, leaving footprints that time cannot erase.

Today, the University of Lagos, the Faculty of Engineering, and the Department of Electrical & Electronics Engineering celebrate two titan scholars, dedicated teachers, inspiring mentors, and enduring pillars of academic distinction: Professor A. I. Mowete and Professor T. O. Akinbulire.

Professor A. I. Mowete

A master of electromagnetic theory and telecommunications engineering whose intellectual rigour, scholarly eloquence, and warm mentorship have guided undergraduates, postgraduates, and academic colleagues across generations. His presence in the lecture hall transformed complex principles into timeless wisdom.

Professor T. O. Akinbulire

A stalwart in electric power systems, machines, and energy engineering whose steadfast dedication to duty, research excellence, and practical problem-solving has strengthened the department's foundations and empowered engineers serving across Nigeria and around the globe.

Through years of selfless service, both professors have contributed not merely to the advancement of technological knowledge, but also to the shaping of character, the nurturing of talent, and the development of future generations of industry leaders, innovators, and educators.

"The influence of great teachers never retires."

Their legacy is not measured solely by the thousands of lectures delivered, research papers published, or academic milestones attained. It is equally reflected in the alumni who lead major technological sectors worldwide, the junior faculty they encouraged, the lives they touched, and the enduring culture of excellence they helped cement at UNILAG.

As we honour them today, we recognise that while a formal chapter of institutional service may draw to a close, their impact will forever resonate in laboratories, lecture halls, national infrastructure, and every future innovation shaped by their teachings.

Professor Mowete and Professor Akinbulire, your service has been a gift, your mentorship a blessing, and your legacy an enduring inspiration to the entire University of Lagos community.

May this golden new chapter bring each of you abundant joy, sound health, renewed peace, well-deserved rest, and the profound fulfilment of seeing the seeds you have sown flourish across the nation and the world.

Congratulations, Professor A. I. Mowete & Professor T. O. Akinbulire!
With heartfelt appreciation for their invaluable contributions to engineering education, research, mentorship, and institutional development.
Prof. P. O. Oluseyi
Head, Department of Electrical & Electronics Engineering, University of Lagos
